01 · CHALLENGE

Full Circle needed an identity as calm as the product itself: one that feels like light rather than data, and that could carry across posters, the companion app and the expo.

How might we make reflection feel warm and inviting instead of clinical?

02 · PROCESS

A vision board, more than 20 poster versions, then a system we could carry across everything.

step 01
Inspiration

A vision board around nature, connection, mental health, well-being, hobbies and self-growth, then color psychology to map feelings to hues.

step 02
Poster iterations

Tested background tones, the shape of the rising peak and taglines, plus bold type on soft orbs and black backgrounds.

03 · FINAL DESIGN

A soft gradient peak that opens into the five pillars: Move, Create, Connect, Restore and Enrich.

01
Color

Soft gradients that feel like light, not ink, with near-black for text.

02
Type

Libre Baskerville for warmth, Libre Franklin for clarity.

03
Illustration

Blurred auras, halos and light trails.

04
Photography

Warm natural light, hands, bodies and landscapes.
