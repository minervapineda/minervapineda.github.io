Festival-level posters and a matching shirt on a student budget: loud, readable from across the courtyard and cheap to print.

01 · CHALLENGE

A poster and matching shirt for Crochella in Crothers Courtyard, April 5th, 3 to 7pm. The brief: a Woodstock, 90s, grunge, Boiler Room vibe, high contrast so it reads from far away, and only 2 to 3 colors to keep printing affordable.

How might we make a student concert feel like a real festival with just a few colors?

02 · PROCESS

Moodboard first, then a lot of versions until the type and colors felt loud enough.

step 01
Moodboard

References from Coachella, Boiler Room, Portola, Lollapalooza and Outside Lands, plus a lot of vintage band tees.

step 02
Iterations

Started with soft gradients, then moved to black backgrounds with loud type and a chrome flower.

problemGradients lacked contrast at a distance.fixBlack base with one pop color (tested red and yellow).
03 · FINAL DESIGN

Big red block letters, a liquid purple version, a warped psychedelic wordmark and a chrome one on black, all on a dark or saturated base with 2 to 3 colors.
