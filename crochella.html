2025

Tools

Posters for Crochella, a festival-themed concert with five student bands.

Design Challenge

The brief was a poster (and a matching shirt) for Crochella in Crothers Courtyard, April 5th, 3 to 7pm. They wanted a Woodstock, 90s, grunge, Boiler Room kind of vibe, high contrast so it reads from far away, and only 2 to 3 colors since more colors get expensive to print.

How might we make a student concert feel like a real festival with just a few colors?

Process

Moodboard first, then a lot of versions until the type and colors felt loud enough.

black base + one pop color

Final Result

4 photos

Final Posters

Four final directions: big red block letters, a liquid purple one, a warped psychedelic wordmark, and a chrome one on black. They all use a dark or saturated base with 2 to 3 colors, so they stay cheap to print and readable from across the courtyard.
