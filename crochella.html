THE BRIEF

Crochella was an afternoon concert in Crothers Courtyard, 3 to 7pm, with five student bands. The organizers wanted a Woodstock, 90s, grunge, Boiler Room feel, with two hard constraints:

  • High contrast, so the poster reads from across the courtyard
  • Only 2 to 3 colors, so it stays cheap to print on posters and shirts
WHAT I DID

research
Built a moodboard of festival posters and merch
iteration
Explored type, color and imagery across many rounds
print
Designed four final poster directions and a matching shirt
THE MOODBOARD

References from Coachella, Boiler Room, Portola, Lollapalooza and Outside Lands, plus a lot of vintage band tees. Most of them shared the same move: a dark base, one loud color and type that takes up the whole page.

moodboard
GETTING LOUD ENOUGH

Soft gradient posters looked good up close but disappeared from far away. I moved to black backgrounds with loud type and a chrome flower, and tested red and yellow as the single pop color.

problemGradients lost contrast at a distance.fixA black base with one pop color.
FOUR FINAL DIRECTIONS

Big red block letters, a liquid purple version, a warped psychedelic wordmark and a chrome one on black. Each uses 2 to 3 colors on a dark or saturated base, so all four stay cheap to print and readable from across the courtyard.
