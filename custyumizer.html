THE CHALLENGE

Plant-forward hospital food is better for patients, often cheaper and far better for the planet. Most hospitals still hesitate. The decision sits with the C-suite, and the case for change has rarely been made in their terms.

How might we help hospital leaders see plant-forward menus as a win for their patients, their budget and the climate?

WHAT I DID

We split the work evenly as a team of five, so I worked on every stage.

interviews
Spoke with chefs, dietitians, food-service leaders and researchers
synthesis
Turned interviews into one core finding and four barriers
strategy
Reframed the case through three leadership lenses
web tool
Designed and built the CustYumizer with Claude Code
RESEARCH
18 experts across hospital food systems

We talked with 18 experts across hospital food systems, including people from Kaiser Permanente, NYC Health + Hospitals, Stanford Medicine, Adventist Health, the Culinary Institute of America, Practice Greenhealth and the American Heart Association.

core finding
There is no one-size-fits-all menu.

What works depends on a hospital’s region, demographics, food system model, funding and priorities.

WHY THE HESITATION

In interview after interview, the same four beliefs came up. Plant-forward food is seen as:

  • Expensive
  • Nutritionally lacking
  • Unappetizing
  • Not core to health

Most of these are misconceptions. The real problem was communication.

THE REFRAME

Three lenses a CEO already thinks in:

01
Patient

Satisfaction, comfort and food that reflects who they serve.

02
Bottom line

Cost per meal and how it fits existing budgets.

03
Impact

Emissions and climate commitments.

THE TOOL
web tool · built with claude code

Enter your hospital’s profile and the CustYumizer builds a plant-forward case in your terms. Each barrier is paired with evidence from hospitals that have already switched. One example is NYC Health + Hospitals: 36% lower greenhouse gas emissions, 98% patient satisfaction against a 74% national average, and $0.59 saved per meal.

WHAT I LEARNED

A good idea is not enough if it is not in the language of the person making the decision. Research told us what to say. Reframing it for hospital leaders told us how to say it.

WHAT’S NEXT

Greener by Default loved the tool, and we are now in conversation with them about taking it further together. Next, we want to put it in front of real hospital food-service leaders and learn which numbers actually change minds.
