01 · CHALLENGE

Plant-forward hospital food is healthier, cheaper and better for the planet, yet most hospitals still hesitate. The decision sits with leadership, and the case for change has never been translated into the language they use.

How might we help hospital leaders see plant-forward menus as a win for patients, budgets and the climate?

insight

The case already exists, but it is not spoken in the C-suite’s language.

barriers
  • Seen as expensive
  • Seen as nutritionally lacking
  • Seen as unappetizing
  • Not seen as core to health
02 · PROCESS

We talked to experts across hospital food systems, then turned what we heard into one core finding and a list of barriers to answer.

step 01
Expert interviews

Chefs, dietitians, food-service leaders and researchers across the country, to learn why menus stall and who makes the call.

step 02
Core finding

Hospitals need tailored solutions shaped by region, demographics, food system model, funding and priorities.

problemEach barrier blocked adoption.fixPaired each one with evidence leaders could act on.
03 · FINAL DESIGN

Enter your hospital’s profile and it builds a plant-forward case in your terms: what it means for patients, the budget and climate impact, backed by hospitals that already switched.
