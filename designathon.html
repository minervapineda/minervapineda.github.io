A playful but official brand for a first-time, two-day design hackathon, built to sit next to partners like Adobe, Figma and Notion.

01 · CHALLENGE

Two days of workshops, speakers and teams designing from a prompt needed a brand that felt playful and creative but still official enough for its partners. I worked on it as part of The Design Kids design team.

How might we make a first-time event feel like something people already want to be part of?

02 · PROCESS

Lots of inspo first, then a brand kit, then the D in every color.

step 01
Inspiration + brand kit

Pulled from conference brands, festival flyers, badges and lanyards, then built a first kit: a bright palette, Helvetica with Times New Roman and early versions of the D mark.

step 02
Logo iterations

Tested the D mark in different color combinations before landing on a white mark over a green, purple and orange gradient.
