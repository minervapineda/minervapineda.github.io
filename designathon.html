Branding for the Stanford Designathon, Stanford’s first-ever design hackathon. Where d.reams meet design.

Design Challenge

The Design Kids were putting on the Designathon, Stanford’s first-ever design hackathon: two days of workshops, speakers and teams designing from a prompt. It needed a brand that felt playful and creative but still official enough to sit next to partners like Adobe, Figma and Notion. I worked on it as part of the design team through The Design Kids.

How might we make a first-time event feel like something people already want to be part of?

Process

Lots of inspo first, then a brand kit, then the D in every color.

d.ream · d.efine · d.o · d.esign
