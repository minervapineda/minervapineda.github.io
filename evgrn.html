THE OPPORTUNITY

evgr.n (say it “evergreen”) is a student-run café that I am helping start from scratch, and it is still very much in the works. East Campus has very few cafés, and EVGR’s Pub is only open a couple of nights a week. We are designing for the people who wake up early: a place to grab coffee and feel inspired, and a place where undergrads and grads finally cross paths.

The café is going into an unused room next to the Pub. The Undergraduate Senate recognized us as an ASSU service organization, and we are now working on permits and fundraising.

WHAT I DO

evgr.n is run by a big student team, and I am one of the people building it from the ground up. As design lead and project manager, I make sure the brand is exactly what we want it to be, and I help keep the whole project moving.

THE IDENTITY

We wanted the brand to feel minimal, clean and colorful: green, a little techie and a little like code.

01
Vision board

Stained glass, mosaic tiles, greenery, warm cafés and a little bit nerdy.

02
Wordmark

A lowercase mono wordmark where a single green square stands in for the dot. That square is the whole brand.

03
Palette

Brand green, a lighter green, near-black and warm off-white, plus blue, light blue, yellow, coral and pink for the mosaic.

04
Type

JetBrains Mono for the logo, labels and prices, and Instrument Sans for sentences and posters.

IN THE WORLD
  • Stickers: the wordmark on every brand color, plus mosaic-bordered versions
  • Loyalty cards: mosaic border on the front, stamp grid on the back
  • Opening flyer: location, hours and the January 2027 target opening
opening flyer
THE SPACE

As the interior designer, I’m shaping the room to feel like a cozy, plant-filled lab: wood floors, white walls, wood vinyl over the grey storage, menus at the entrance and a Vestaboard with a riddle of the day.
