01 · CHALLENGE

My jewelry box was too small, and most stands sold online were too bulky for my minimal room. I wanted something functional, visually minimal and compact enough for a nightstand.

How might we design a jewelry stand that organizes everything without overwhelming a small space?

directions explored
  • Tree-inspired: too irregular and cluttered
  • Minimal hook: not enough space
  • Tiered circular: too bulky, better for storage
  • Interlocking: strongest, chosen and refined
requirements
  • Holds rings, earrings, bangles and necklaces
  • Fits a nightstand
  • Assembles by hand
  • Looks good empty or full
02 · PROCESS

Cardboard first, then fully constrained CAD, a render to check proportions, and then the laser cutter.

step 01
Cardboard prototype

Traced and cut the interlocking profile to test proportions, height and how the two pieces slot together.

step 02
CAD + constraints

Fully constrained the curved profile and octagonal base in Fusion 360, with R1.289 arcs, a 3.5 in stand height and slot placement matched to plywood thickness.

step 03
Rendering + assembly

Rendered the stand, base and full assembly in plywood to check proportions and fit before cutting.

step 04
Fabrication

Laser-cut from plywood, then test-fit the slots.

problemStand fit too tight or too loose in the base.fixRefined slot widths for kerf and material thickness.
problemBase came out too large; some parts did not cut through.fixAdjusted scale and cut settings, then recut.
