01 · CHALLENGE

Redesign a tool that a lot of people use every day but that is not ergonomic for everyone. We focused on people without much strength in their hands, where a regular lighter takes far more thumb force than it should.

How might we make a lighter that is easy and safe to use with limited grip strength?

what testing showed
  • Too much thumb force
  • Hand ends up close to the flame
  • Poor wrist rotation
  • Flame direction is not obvious
  • Frequent users get wrist strain from repetition
requirements
  • Less wrist strain
  • Longer nozzle for safety
  • Clear flame direction
  • A better grip for most hands

step 04
Ergonomic analysis

Lighters are not designed for different hand sizes or long use. People with arthritis had a harder time keeping a grip.

problemHigh thumb force and awkward wrist movement.fixPalm grip that spreads force across the whole hand.
problemHand too close to the flame.fixLonger nozzle.
